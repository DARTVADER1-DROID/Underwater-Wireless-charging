# Research and Formula Notes

This folder separates published research and sourced equations from the project's own design assumptions and calculations.

## Structure

- [Papers](papers/README.md): paper-by-paper technical summaries, extracted parameters, limitations and relevance to this prototype.
- [Formula notes](formulas/README.md): formula provenance, definitions, units, assumptions and applicability.

## Attribution and use

The notes paraphrase and summarize research; they do not replace the original papers. Always consult and cite the original publication before relying on a formula or reported result.

The Chen et al. underwater WPT paper is marked by SAGE as Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0). The project notes attribute the authors and DOI. Do not copy figures or long passages without preserving the applicable attribution and licence notices.

## Critical separation

A published result is not automatically a design requirement for this prototype. For example, the Chen et al. paper's tile-shaped coils, ferrite backing, 30 kHz seawater simulation and 6-turn Litz prototype are research-reference data. They are not the dimensions or frequency selected for our 7-turn circular solid-wire prototype.
